operational companion to the education catalog and is updated once per

## How to use this card

1. Confirm the requested product and cycle against the education catalog.
2. Check availability in the destination market before quoting a lead time.
3. For cross-region sourcing, use the reference amount recorded on the
   catalog card and append the program note for the cycle.

## Order intake

Purchase orders are accepted through the partner portal until 16:00 local
warehouse time. Orders above 40 units require a factory allocation check;
the desk confirms allocation within one business day. Partial shipments
are allowed only for split destination markets and must be noted on the
purchase order.

## Returns and DOA handling

Dead-on-arrival units are replaced from the regional warehouse within five
business days. The partner must attach a photo of the serial label and the
original packing list. Returns for non-DOA reasons are subject to a
restocking review by channel-ops.

## Escalations

Same-day escalations go to the channel-ops duty queue. Include the partner
account, destination market and requested quantity. Stock holds expire after
two business days.

## Cycle notes

Quarterly rate cards are published by treasury on the first working day of
the cycle. The reseller desk does not maintain its own price records;
always reference the catalog repository for the current cycle.
